How Long Will My Clubs Take?

Total estimated delivery: 2 to 4 weeks from the day you order.

Drive Fresh ships direct from international fulfillment partners. That is how we keep prices far below pro-shop retail, and it is also why delivery takes longer than a domestic warehouse. We would rather be upfront about that than surprise you later.

Processing

Orders are prepared and dispatched within 5 to 7 business days. Orders placed on a weekend or holiday begin processing the next business day.

Transit

Estimated transit is 12 to 21 days after dispatch, depending on your destination and customs clearance.

Tracking

Every order ships with tracking. You will get a tracking number by email as soon as your order dispatches. Tracking often takes 3 to 5 days to start updating after dispatch — that is normal and does not mean anything is wrong.

Customs and Duties

International shipments may be held for customs clearance, which can add time. Any import duties or taxes charged by your country are the responsibility of the customer.

Peak Season

During Black Friday and the December holidays, please allow up to an additional 7 days.

Packaging and Insurance

Clubs are packed in reinforced boxes and shipped insured against loss and damage in transit. Please inspect your package on arrival and contact us within 7 days of delivery if there is any damage.

Shipping Rates

Rates are calculated and shown at checkout before you pay.

Order Not Arrived?

If your order has not arrived within the estimated window, email us with your order number and we will track it down for you.
