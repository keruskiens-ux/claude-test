All Sales Are Final

Golf Empire does not accept change-of-mind returns or exchanges. Because our equipment ships directly from international fulfillment partners, we cannot process returns for buyer's remorse, incorrect size or flex selection, or a change of plans after ordering.

Please read the full specifications on each product page before ordering, including loft, flex, club count and handedness. If you are unsure which specification suits your swing, email us before you buy and we will help you choose. We would much rather answer a question than have you receive something that does not fit your game.

What We Do Cover

Final sale does not mean no protection. We stand behind every order in the following situations, and in each case we pay all costs:

Damaged in Transit

If your equipment arrives physically damaged, photograph the shipping box and the damaged club before unpacking further and email the photos to us within 7 days of delivery. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund, your choice. Every order ships insured.

Defective on Arrival

If a club has a manufacturing defect — a loose head, a cracked face, a bonding failure — contact us within 30 days of delivery with photos. We will replace it or refund it in full.

Not as Described

If what arrives does not match the specifications on the product page — wrong loft, wrong flex, wrong number of clubs, materially different construction — we will correct it at no cost to you. You do not pay return shipping in this situation.

Never Delivered

If your order does not arrive and tracking cannot confirm delivery, we will refund you in full or reship at your choice. Contact us once the estimated delivery window has fully passed.

Shipping Delays Beyond Our Stated Window

Our stated delivery window is 2 to 4 weeks. If we cannot ship your order within that window, you are entitled to cancel for a full refund. We will contact you if a delay occurs and offer you that choice.

Order Cancellations

You may cancel any order for a full refund at any time before it is dispatched — normally the first 1 to 3 business days after you order. Email us with your order number. Once an order has left the fulfillment center it cannot be cancelled.

Customers in the European Union and United Kingdom

If you are purchasing from the EU or UK, consumer law gives you a statutory right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it, for any reason. That right applies regardless of this policy. To exercise it, email us within 14 days of delivery. The goods must be returned unused and in their original condition, and the cost of return shipping is yours. We will refund the purchase price within 14 days of receiving the returned goods.

Your Statutory Rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the consumer protection laws of your country, state or province. Where local law grants you a right that this policy does not, that law applies.
